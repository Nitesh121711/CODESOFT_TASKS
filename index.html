What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack in which attackers try to trick users into revealing sensitive information or interacting with fraudulent content.

⚠️ Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs or other sensitive information with unknown or suspicious sources.

🔍 How to Identify Phishing

📧 Suspicious Emails

Check the sender address, unexpected attachments, urgent requests and unusual links.

🔐 Fake Login Pages

Check the website address carefully before entering your username or password.

🌐 Fraudulent Websites

Look for suspicious domains, spelling mistakes, unusual requests and misleading content.

🎭 Common Social Engineering Techniques

  • Urgency: Creating pressure to make you act quickly.
  • Impersonation: Pretending to be a trusted person or organization.
  • Fear: Threatening account suspension or other consequences.
  • Reward: Offering fake prizes, jobs or benefits.
  • Trust: Using familiar names, logos or relationships to appear legitimate.

🛡️ Security Tips

✅ Verify the sender before responding.

✅ Check URLs carefully before opening them.

✅ Avoid clicking suspicious links or attachments.

✅ Use strong and unique passwords.

✅ Enable multi-factor authentication when available.

✅ Keep your operating system and applications updated.

✅ Report suspicious messages instead of forwarding them.

📚 Case Study

Imagine receiving an email claiming that your account will be suspended unless you click a link and verify your details. The message creates urgency and asks for sensitive information.

Before taking action, verify the message through the organization's official website or another trusted channel.
